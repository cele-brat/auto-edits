From memory card to delivery in four steps

Each step runs on the server. Your computer only shows you the result.

  1. Add photos

    Pick the wedding's folder. Raw files stay on your computer: only the preview inside each one is uploaded. Sub-folders such as Haldi or Sangeet become event folders.

  2. Cull

    Every photo is rated Keep, Maybe or Reject, with the reason shown. Change any rating with one key.

  3. Edit

    Choose one of 500 built-in presets, or your own, for each event folder, tick what should be worked out per photo, and fine-tune any single frame.

  4. Export

    Pick the size, quality and watermark. The server makes the JPEGs and hands you one zip.

Culling that explains itself

The server looks at each frame the way you would on a contact sheet, and tells you why it marked it.

  • Six checks. Focus, motion blur, exposure, closed eyes, awkward expressions and near-identical shots.
  • Three strictness levels. Relaxed rejects only clear failures. Strict keeps fewer.
  • Best of a burst. Among near-identical frames it can keep the sharpest one with eyes open.
  • Your word is final. Press 3, 2 or 1 to rate a photo yourself. A new cull never changes your ratings, and nothing is ever deleted.

500 presets or your own, with every frame corrected on its own

A preset gives the look. The automatic corrections make each photo fit it, so a dark frame and a bright frame come out matching.

  • 500 presets built in. 100 looks, each in five variations, across six packs: Portrait, Glamour, Studio, Street, Landscape and World.
  • Bring your own presets. Upload the .xmp files you exported from Lightroom. Masks saved inside a preset come with it.
  • Automatic, per photo. Exposure, white balance, highlights, shadows, whites, blacks, noise, straightening and lens fix. Tick only the ones you want.
  • White balance that reads skin. It uses neutral areas and faces together, so mixed wedding light does not turn people orange or green.
  • Whole folder or one photo. Set an event once, then adjust any single frame without touching the rest.

One app, two ways to finish

Use it on its own for speed, or with Lightroom Classic when the final files must come from your catalogue.

On its own

For culling, proofs, social posts and quick delivery. No Lightroom needed.

  • Add a folder straight from the browser
  • Cull, edit and export on the server
  • Download the finished JPEGs as one zip

With Lightroom Classic

For finals that must match Lightroom exactly. A plug-in connects your catalogue to the app.

  • Send selected photos from Lightroom in one step
  • Choose looks in the app, on Lightroom's own previews
  • Bring the edits back as normal develop settings, with a "Before Auto Edits" snapshot on every photo
