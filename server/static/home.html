From memory card to delivery in four steps

Each step runs on the server. Your computer only shows you the result.

  1. Add photos

    Pick the wedding's folder. Raw files stay on your computer: only the JPEG preview inside each one is uploaded. Sub-folders such as Haldi or Sangeet become event folders.

  2. Cull

    Every photo is rated Keep, Maybe or Reject, with the reason shown. Change any rating with one key.

  3. Edit

    Choose one of 500 built-in presets, or your own, for each event folder, tick what should be worked out per photo, and fine-tune any single frame.

  4. Export

    Pick the size, quality and watermark. The server makes the JPEGs from the uploaded previews and hands you one zip. For finals from the RAW, send the edits back to Lightroom.

Culling that explains itself

The server looks at each frame the way you would on a contact sheet, and tells you why it marked it.

  • Six checks. Focus, motion blur, exposure, closed eyes, awkward expressions and near-identical shots.
  • Three strictness levels. Relaxed rejects only clear failures. Strict keeps fewer.
  • Best of a burst. Among near-identical frames it can keep the sharpest one with eyes open.
  • Your word is final. Press 3, 2 or 1 to rate a photo yourself. A new cull never changes your ratings, and nothing is ever deleted.

Questions photographers ask

What is Auto Edits?

Auto Edits is an online tool for wedding photographers. It culls a whole wedding, applies your Lightroom presets with corrections worked out for each photo, and exports the finished JPEGs on a server, so your own computer stays free.

Are my raw files uploaded?

No. Raw files stay on your computer. Only the preview stored inside each raw file is uploaded, which keeps uploads fast and your originals at home.

Are exports as sharp as Lightroom's?

Exports made in the app come from the JPEG preview that was uploaded, not from the raw file. That is right for proofs, social media and quick delivery, and an export is never larger than its preview (most cameras store a preview of 1600 to 6000 px; "Full size" keeps all of it). For the sharpest finals, use the Lightroom route: the plug-in brings your edits back into Lightroom Classic, which applies them to the raw file.

Does it work with Lightroom Classic?

Yes. A Lightroom Classic plug-in sends selected photos to the app and brings the edits back into your catalogue as normal develop settings, with a "Before Auto Edits" snapshot on every photo. You can also use Auto Edits on its own, without Lightroom.

Can I use my own presets?

Yes. Upload the .xmp presets you exported from Lightroom, including presets with masks. Auto Edits also has 500 presets built in: 100 looks, each in five variations.

How does the automatic culling decide?

Each photo is checked for focus, motion blur, exposure, closed eyes, awkward expressions and near-identical shots, then rated Keep, Maybe or Reject with the reason shown. You can change any rating yourself, and nothing is ever deleted.

How much does Auto Edits cost?

You pay with credits. Each photo you export, or send back to Lightroom with its edit, is one credit. Culling is one credit for every 10 photos. A credit costs ₹1 until 31 December 2026 and ₹3 after that, plus 18% GST. Credits are sold from 500 (₹590 with GST).

Is there a free trial?

Yes. New accounts get 50 free credits once the email address is confirmed, with no card needed. Free credits can be used for 15 days; credits you buy never expire. That is enough to export 50 edited photos or cull 500. There is one free trial per internet connection.

What sizes can I export?

2560 px for proofs and social media, 4000 px, or full size (as large as the uploaded preview), at JPEG quality 80, 90 or 95. You can add a text watermark and export every photo, only Keep, or Keep and Maybe.

Can it learn my editing style?

Yes. In the Presets panel choose “+” › “Learn my style…” and give it the Lightroom .xmp sidecar files of photos you edited before (in Lightroom: select them, then Metadata › Save Metadata to File). Auto Edits groups your edits into up to five styles and makes a preset for each, with a plain summary of how you edit. Add the original previews with matching names and it also learns one preset per kind of light: bright outdoor, indoor tungsten, dusk. It costs nothing.

Can my clients pick their favourites?

Yes. “Share as gallery…” makes a private link (with an optional password, open for 30 days by default) where the couple sees the edited previews, hearts their favourites, leaves comments and sends the selection. You get an email, and the picks show in the app under a “Client picks” filter. Galleries are free.

How do I install the Lightroom plug-in?

Download the plug-in and unzip it. In Lightroom Classic choose File › Plug-in Manager › Add and pick the AutoEdits.lrplugin folder. Then make your key in the app (Account › Lightroom plug-in) and paste it in Library › Plug-in Extras › Auto Edits: server settings.

Are my photos private?

Yes. Nothing is visible without logging in, each photographer sees only their own weddings, and the connection is encrypted.
